## 5. FEATURES
====================================================
1. Home: big module tiles (icon + Bangla + English), progress rings, parent-corner button.
2. Module → Section tiles with progress. Section → grid of cut-out cards (picture large, name under it). Tap → Flashcard.
3. Flashcard: huge cut-out with soft shadow, English word (colored, bold), Bengali pronunciation in parentheses, Bengali meaning large. Swipe + arrows, Auto-play mode, tap picture = bounce + speak.

7.
